Can a coupled “mechanics + perfusion” model—parameterized by Brillouin-derived elastic moduli—explain dysregulated neurovascular coupling in clinical conditions (e.g., heart failure, Tourette syndrome) better than hemodynamic data alone, yielding new mechanistic biomarkers?
