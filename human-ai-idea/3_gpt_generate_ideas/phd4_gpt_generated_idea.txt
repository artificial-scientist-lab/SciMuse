Create a live-cell optical platform that couples quantum emitters in non-Hermitian (exceptional-point) nanophotonic environments to super-resolution imaging, enabling ultrasensitive, real-time tracking and quantification of coronavirus RNA localization and glycocalyx-dependent uptake pathways.

Research Question & Novel Results (2 points):  
1) Can exceptional-point–engineered emitter–photonic structures transduce nanoscale changes in local refractive index/absorption caused by viral RNA complexes and cell-surface glycoRNA domains into measurably amplified, single-photon-level signals for label-free (or minimally labeled) detection in living cells?  
2) How do pharmacological perturbations of glycosylation and spatially controlled “paintbrush” delivery of inhibitors/nanoparticles reshape the spatiotemporal coupling between viral RNA trafficking and glycocalyx-mediated entry routes, as revealed by correlated super-resolution maps and quantum-emitter emission statistics (spectral shifts, linewidth changes, photon correlations)?
