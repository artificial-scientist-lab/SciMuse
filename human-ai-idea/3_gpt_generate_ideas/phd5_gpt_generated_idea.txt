Project Title:  
**Brillouin Neurohemodynamics: Label‑Free Mapping of Brain Microvascular Mechanics to Explain Fast CBF/BOLD Sign Changes**

Main Objective (1-2 sentences):  
Develop and validate a Brillouin-scattering–based optical platform to quantify microscale viscoelastic properties of cerebral microvessels and surrounding parenchyma, and link these mechanical metrics to dynamic cerebral blood flow (CBF) and BOLD responses measured with multi-echo fMRI/PET.

Research Question & Novel Results (2 points):  
1) **Do rapid, region-specific changes in microvascular and perivascular stiffness (measured by Brillouin frequency shifts) predict the occurrence and magnitude of positive vs. negative CBF/BOLD responses during stress or task paradigms?**  
2) **Can a coupled “mechanics + perfusion” model—parameterized by Brillouin-derived elastic moduli—explain dysregulated neurovascular coupling in clinical conditions (e.g., heart failure, Tourette syndrome) better than hemodynamic data alone, yielding new mechanistic biomarkers?**
